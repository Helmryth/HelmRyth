Helmryth Mobile is the private iPhone surface for Helmryth on your workbench.

Keep your operators moving when you step away from your desk:

• Answer gates and questions
• Follow replies as they stream
• Send messages to operators and crews
• Switch and manage run contexts
• Search the complete local transcript history
• Edit messages and move between conversation versions
• Share transcripts as Markdown or JSON
• Watch an operator's workbench when you need context

YOUR WORKBENCH STAYS IN CHARGE

Helmryth Mobile connects to Helmryth Reach on your own workbench. Your operator transcripts remain in Helmryth's local storage; the optional hosted connection transports them to your workbench without creating a developer-hosted transcript copy.

PAIR ONCE, REVOKE ANY TIME

Pair with a short-lived code from Helmryth's Reach panel. The workbench stores only a hash of the phone credential and lets you revoke the device at any time.

USE IT AT HOME OR AWAY

Bonjour finds your workbench on a trusted local network. For remote access, use Tailscale on both devices or enable the optional email-verified HTTPS connection in Helmryth's desktop Reach settings.

Requires Helmryth running on a Mac, Windows, or Linux workbench. Tailscale is optional and is not affiliated with Helmryth.
